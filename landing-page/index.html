Taste the Authentic Flavors of The Gambia

From rich Domoda to flavorful Benachin, we bring warm, home-cooked Gambian dishes straight to your table.

Our Signature Dishes

Domoda

Rich peanut stew cooked with tender meat or chicken, served over steamed rice.

Benachin

One-pot rice prepared with fresh vegetables, spices, and your choice of fish or chicken.

Yassa

Tangy marinated chicken cooked with caramelized onions, mustard, and lime.

Fufu

Soft fufu served with a rich, savory soup and traditional spices.
